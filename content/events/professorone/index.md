**Arriva il professorone** ("Here comes the big professor") was an evening of science outreach at ARCI L'Impegno in Milan, part of a series of science-communication events.

I talked about cosmology while the cartoonist **Davide La Rosa** drew, live and on a big screen, the situations I was describing. The host, **Davide Arcuri**, asked me questions, and so did the audience.
